University of Livingstonia · Rumphi

Find your student transport

Choose a campus pickup point and travel to Rumphi or Mzuzu, or find a vehicle returning from Mzuzu to campus.

How the local service works: For journeys from campus to Mzuzu, vehicles may depart around 6:00 AM and arrive around 10:00–11:00 AM. On the return journey, students meet vehicles at Pama BIN in Mzuzu. Drivers drop passengers at the selected campus points listed above. The shared fare guide is MWK 30,000–35,000 including bags. Confirm the exact fare, pickup time and seat with the driver before travelling.
